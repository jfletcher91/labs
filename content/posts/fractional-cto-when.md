Early-stage founders often wait too long to bring in technical leadership, or hire a full-time CTO too early. A fractional CTO bridges that gap.

## Five signals it's time

1. **You're choosing a tech stack** and nobody on the team has scaled one before.
2. **An agency is building your product** and you can't evaluate their work.
3. **Investors are asking technical diligence questions** you can't answer confidently.
4. **You're about to hire engineers** and need someone to interview them.
5. **Your product is slowing down** and every feature takes longer than the last.

## What a good engagement looks like

Expect two to three days a month to start: architecture reviews, roadmap planning, hiring support, and a clear plan for when to hire a full-time leader.
