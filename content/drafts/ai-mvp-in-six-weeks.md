Most AI products don't fail because the model is weak. They fail because the team spent six months polishing a demo nobody asked for. Here's the six-week cadence we use with founders.

## Week 1: Pick one painful job

Interview ten target users and find the task they complain about most. Write it down as a single sentence: *"Help a ___ do ___ in half the time."*

## Weeks 2–3: Prototype the core loop

- Start with an off-the-shelf model and prompt engineering before any fine-tuning.
- Put a human in the loop for anything high-stakes.
- Log every input and output so you can measure quality from day one.

## Weeks 4–5: Put it in front of users

Ship to five design partners. Watch them use it live. Fix what confuses them, not what you think is missing.

## Week 6: Decide with data

Look at retention, time saved, and willingness to pay. Then choose: double down, pivot the use case, or stop.

> The goal of an MVP isn't to impress investors. It's to learn faster than your runway shrinks.
